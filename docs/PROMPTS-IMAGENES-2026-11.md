# Prompts utilizados — CePIA

Herramienta: image_gen integrada. MMICs se usó como referencia de iluminación, color y acabado para las otras piezas; nunca como referencia de contenido.

## Base

Create a photorealistic high-end editorial still life for CePIA radio astronomy lab website. Generic illustrative technology, never a replica of CePIA proprietary equipment. Vertical 4:5 image, output high resolution. Cinematic consistent series: deep petrol #1B3346 upper glow fading to night #0F1012. Aqua #9FE6E3 rim from behind above, subtle opposing low orange #F58E34 rim, dim warm #FFE2C4 key. Real machined materials, controlled soft reflections, AgX-like highlight rolloff, gentle S curve, restrained vignette and fine grain. Low close three-quarter 20-24mm viewpoint, coherent perspective, no fisheye. Subject entirely visible in upper 60%, breathing room, calm empty top-left, lower 40% near-black empty for text. For object subjects suspend freely without floor/support/contact shadow; settings explicitly override this. Sharp central subject, subtle edge depth of field, bloom only brightest highlights. No text, numbers, logos, labels, people, hands, flags, watermarks, neon, sci-fi glow or warped geometry. Output only finished image.

## mmics

An open gold-plated split-block millimetre-wave receiver module around 100 GHz, top half lifted away. Lower half has a precisely machined rectangular waveguide channel, a tiny dark MMIC chip connected by hair-thin gold wire bonds, small ceramic substrates and two miniature coaxial connectors on its side. Nearest corner projects toward lens and waveguide leads diagonally into frame. The two halves form one assembly. Warm gold against aqua rim.

## pixel-3mm

A single-pixel millimetre-wave receiver assembly: satin machined aluminium corrugated feedhorn with fine concentric grooves visible inside its circular aperture, mounted on a compact gold-plated block. Horn aperture nearest lens, body recedes diagonally, tactile machining marks.

## mkid

A thin round silicon wafer bearing a dense precise array of microscopic superconducting detector pixels: repeating square cells with fine meandering lines. Strongly tilted, near edge large and sharp, far edge soft. Subtle thin-film aqua and faint gold iridescence, no saturated violet. Thin mirror-like wafer edge.

## integracion-hibrida

A compact hybrid integration assembly: several small chips and passive components bonded side by side on a ceramic carrier inside a gold-plated housing. Short arcs of gold wire bonds and fine microstrip lines. Macro view nearest chip and bonds large and crisp, carrier recedes diagonally.

## grte

Near dish rim large, mount recedes. Utilitarian visible bolts and painted steel, credible geometry. Whole assembly floating freely against dark void, no ground.

## code
